Example 34 (Slide 138)

Nominal Interest Rate - Many banks, for example, state the interest arrangement for credit cards in this way: “18% compounded monthly.” - We say 18% is the nominal interest rate or annual percentage rate (APR), and the compounding frequency is monthly (12). - To obtain the interest rate per compounding period, we divide 18% by 12 to obtain 1.5% per month. Therefore, the credit card statement above means that the bank will charge 1.5% interest on unpaid balance for each month. 138
