Example 45 (Slide 165)

Consider the investment cash flows associated with the metal fabrication project in previous example If the firm’s MARR is 15%, compute the NPW of this project? Is this project acceptable (PW (i) > 0)? Solution: We have an initial investment of $1,250,000 at n = 0 followed by the 15 equal annual savings of $731,500, and $80,000 salvage value at the end of 15 years, we determine the NPW as follows: PWOutflow (15%) = $1,250,000 PWInflow (15%) = $731,500(P/A, 15%, 15) + $80,000(P/F, 15%, 15) = $4,284,259 NPW (15%) = PWInflows (15%) - PWOutflows (15%) = $4,284,259 - $1,250,000 = $3,034,259 165 Example Net Present Worth
