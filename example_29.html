Example 29 (Slide 121)

The answer depends on what rate of interest you could earn on any money you receive today. For example, if you could deposit the $1,000 today at 12% per year, you would prefer to be paid today. Alternatively, if you could only earn 5% on deposited funds, you would be better off if you chose the $1,100 in one year. 121 Time Value of Money [2]
