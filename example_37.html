Example Calculating Auto Loan Payments Suppose you want to buy a car priced $22,678.95. The car dealer is willing to offer a financing package with 8.5% annual percentage rate (compounded monthly) over 48 months. You can afford to make a down payment of $2678.95, so the net amount to be financed is $20,000. What would be the monthly payment? Solution In automobile financing the interest and the payment periods are almost always both monthly. Thus, the 8.5% APR means 8.5% compounded monthly.
