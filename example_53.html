184 Example To meet increased sales, a large dairy is planning to purchase 10 new delivery trucks. Each truck will cost $18,000. Compute the depreciation schedule for each truck, using the modified accelerated cost recovery system (MACRS) method, if the recovery period is 5 years. Solution: Year Depreciation ($) 1 18,000(.20) 3,600.00 2 18,000(.32) 5,760.00 3 18,000(.192) 3,456.00 4 18,000(.1152) 2,073.60 5 18,000(.1152) 2,073.60 6 18,000(.0576) 1,036.80
